## No AI-Generated Text in Issues and Pull Requests

Please write all issue descriptions, pull request summaries and review replies in your own words. Do not paste unedited output from LLMs (such as ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, ...). 

* **Why?** AI-generated texts are often overly verbose, hallucinate context, and create disproportionate review overhead for maintainers.
* **Consequence:** Issues or PRs with obvious, unedited AI-generated descriptions or boilerplate summaries will be closed without review. If you use AI tools for translation or grammar, keep your text concise and verify every single statement before posting.
